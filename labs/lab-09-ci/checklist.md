# Lab 9 - Done when

- [ ] Actions are enabled on your fork, and `run-tests.yml` ran on your push.
- [ ] Your pull request is on your fork, and its test check failed.
- [ ] The pull request has a triage comment: an agent's root cause, or the `robotcode results` summary without a key.
- [ ] You can say whether the comment's root cause matches what you broke, and which evidence it gave.
- [ ] The pull request is closed, not merged, and `main` on your fork is unchanged.
